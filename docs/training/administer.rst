2. Administer the items
=======================

Use the manual and the study condition together
-----------------------------------------------

The following administration rules come from manual p. 2. The approved study
instructions determine how these rules are delivered through the assigned
condition. Keep a record of any approved modification rather than presenting
it as the original manual procedure. See :doc:`sources`.

Practice first
--------------

The manual permits feedback and encouragement during the practice sentence.
Practice can be re-administered when the child does not understand the task or
needs to be redirected to an imitative response.

Bloom currently provides 16 scored items and has no separate practice-item
control. Use the practice material and delivery procedure supplied by the study
lead while continuous recording is running. **Do not turn scored item 1 into an
unscored practice item or invent a practice stimulus for participant use.**

Keep the approved introduction and practice procedure consistent across the
assigned condition. Do not improvise extra coaching during the scored items.

One item, from prompt to final answer
-------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 40 42

   * - Step
     - What the researcher does
     - Bloom control or confirmation
   * - 1. Present
     - Deliver the exact assigned stimulus through the condition's specified speaker.
     - **Cue examiner prompt** for examiner delivery; **Play prompt** for browser or M delivery.
   * - 2. Listen
     - Listen for the audible end of the prompt. An M queue message is not proof that playback has finished.
     - Confirm actual delivery before proceeding.
   * - 3. Mark
     - Mark the response window after the prompt ends. Continue listening for the child's response and any revision.
     - **Prompt ended / start response window**.
   * - 4. Finish the attempt
     - Accept the child's final answer, including a revision from correct to incorrect. Do not select the best earlier answer.
     - **End attempt**, after the final response.
   * - 5. Continue
     - Select the next numbered item and repeat the sequence until all 16 have been administered.
     - Numbered item buttons; recording remains on.

Response markers annotate the continuous recording. They do not start or stop it.
If speech begins early, overlaps the prompt, or is difficult to segment, preserve
the audio and document the event. Review the response window before analysis;
do not ask for an extra response simply to make the segmentation easier.

When a child asks for a repeat
------------------------------

Manual p. 2 allows **one repetition of each test sentence** when the child requests
it or the examiner considers it appropriate, such as after an interruption.

End the current attempt before replaying, document the reason, and present the
same item again through the assigned delivery mode. Track whether that item's
one allowed repetition has been used. Bloom does not automatically enforce the
manual's repetition limit. More available software attempts do not mean more
protocol-authorized repetitions.

Bloom analyzes the latest completed, non-excluded response window for each item.
That software selection rule is not permission to keep trying until an answer
improves. Preserve every attempt and review the child's final response.

When there is no response
-------------------------

For silence or an indication that the child cannot remember, the manual provides
this prompt: **“Just tell me what you can remember.”** If there is still no response,
record a no-response outcome in the zero-point category described by the manual.
Document what happened; do not confuse a genuine no-response trial with missing
or inaudible recording data. See :doc:`review` for the manual's category-label issue.

Do not invent a fixed wait interval: the supplied manual does not specify one.
Use a timing rule only if the study lead has supplied it in the study instructions.

Do not stop because answers are incorrect
-----------------------------------------

Administer all 16 test items regardless of response accuracy. A sequence of errors
is not an accuracy-based stopping rule in this manual. If the session ends for
another reason, follow the study's stopping procedures and identify it as
incomplete instead of filling unadministered items with zeroes.

Breaks and interruptions
------------------------

End any open attempt before choosing **Break (keep recording)**. Select **Resume**
when ready. The child display changes, but audio capture continues. Follow the
study's break policy; the software button does not define that policy.

Do not refresh, navigate away from, close, or sign out of the researcher page
while recording. A browser or microphone interruption ends continuous capture;
recover the available record and report the interruption rather than claiming
that the recording continued. See :doc:`quick-reference`.
