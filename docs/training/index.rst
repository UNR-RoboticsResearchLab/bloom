Researcher training
===================

Learn to administer the RSR study in Bloom, preserve the full interaction, and
review responses using the supplied Redmond Sentence Recall manual.

**Audience:** researchers administering or reviewing study sessions.
**Format:** guided reading, adult role-play, scoring practice, and trainer review.
Allow about 45-60 minutes for an initial training session, plus a rehearsal with
M before administering a robot condition. This is a suggested training schedule,
not the screening duration specified by the manual.

Start here
----------

1. Read :doc:`prepare` and confirm the study's condition instructions with the lead.
2. Work through :doc:`administer` and :doc:`review` with the manual available.
3. Complete :doc:`practice` with an adult colleague using a training participant code.
4. Use :doc:`readiness` with a trainer before independent administration.
5. Keep :doc:`quick-reference` open during later sessions.

.. important::

   The supplied manual describes headphone-based administration. Bloom offers
   examiner, browser, and M delivery modes, but these default mappings do not
   define the three approved study conditions. The study lead must supply the
   condition assignments, scripts, practice stimulus, and any approved departures
   from the manual. Record these in :doc:`protocol` before participant use.
