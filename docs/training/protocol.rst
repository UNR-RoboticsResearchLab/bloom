Study-specific condition sheet
==============================

The manual provides administration and scoring procedures. It does **not**
define this study's three conditions, allocation procedure, consent/assent
materials, or robot adaptations. The study lead completes this working sheet
before participant administration and records the applicable protocol version.
No condition descriptions or approvals have been supplied in this documentation.

Decisions to record
-------------------

* **Version and authorization:** responsible lead, protocol/version, date, and
  the study's actual authorization for each adaptation.
* **Assignment:** how the researcher obtains the participant's assigned condition,
  and the item/session order to follow. Do not create a new allocation method.
* **Introduction and practice:** exact approved wording, practice stimulus,
  delivery method, feedback, and how to repeat or revisit practice. Bloom has no
  dedicated practice-item control.
* **Auditory setup:** headphones/speakers, microphone location, volume check,
  which devices may speak, and how M's actual delivery is confirmed.
* **Repeats and prompts:** whether the manual's one-repeat rule and no-response
  prompt apply unchanged. Identify any approved changes and their rationale.
* **Pacing and breaks:** any study-specific wait interval, break procedure, and
  response to a child's request to stop. Do not attribute an invented time limit
  or break rule to the manual.
* **Scoring:** designated reviewers, treatment of the manual's four-error label
  inconsistency, dialect judgments, uncertain utterances, and disagreements.
* **Age and interpretation:** eligibility, applicable age bands, preselected
  cutoff, and handling of ages beyond the supplied tables or incomplete sessions.
* **Reporting:** how reviewed totals, unchanged automatic decisions, and
  departures from the original auditory administration are reported.
* **Recording and access:** capture start/finish, authorized locations, archive
  responsibility, permitted exports, retention, and incident reporting.
* **Interruption recovery:** who decides whether to continue, repeat an item,
  create a separate session, or end an incomplete session. Preserve the original
  record and identify any gap or deviation.
* **Training clearance:** conditions each researcher must rehearse, the lead's
  completion criteria, trainer, and training-record location.

Implementation checks
---------------------

Before describing the workflow as implementing the study protocol, explicitly
reconcile these current software behaviors:

* No separate practice stimulus or practice-item workflow is built into the
  16-item interface.
* Replays are available without an automatic one-repeat limit.
* ASR chooses the latest completed, non-excluded window for an item; human review
  must establish the child's final answer rather than selecting the best attempt.
* Score overrides retain the automatic transcript and do not recalculate the
  automatic screening decision.
* The age-entry range extends beyond the last age band in the supplied manual.
* A local/browser recovery copy is separate from the server archive.

This sheet records decisions; it does not grant approval or modify the manual.
