Session quick reference
=======================

Use alongside the approved script, condition sheet, and RSR manual. For an
explanation of a step, return to :doc:`index`.

Before recording
----------------

* Confirm the study's permission/assent process, participant code, age, cutoff,
  assigned condition, and correct researcher account.
* Prepare the approved introduction and practice material.
* Check microphone placement and the child display; pair M when applicable.
* Confirm the correct speaker and that **Child display connected** is shown.

Start before speaking
---------------------

**Start continuous recording → verify running → approved introduction and practice.**

A child may speak before the first prompt. Record that speech too.

For every scored item
---------------------

**Cue/play prompt → hear its end → mark response window → hear final answer →
End attempt → next numbered item.**

* Administer all 16 items regardless of accuracy.
* Allow one test-sentence repetition when requested or warranted under manual p. 2;
  track usage yourself, because Bloom does not limit replay clicks.
* For no response or failure to remember, use **“Just tell me what you can
  remember.”** Document continued no response separately from missing audio.
* Score the final answer, not the best answer.
* End an open attempt before **Break (keep recording)**; use **Resume** afterward.

Finish and save
---------------

**Approved closing → Finish recording → listen and check windows → analyze →
review scores → Save archive to Bloom → confirm retrieval → sign out.**

* Correct or exclude response windows with reasons before successful analysis.
* Apply the manual's contraction, dialect, dysfluency, and transposition rules.
* Use the documented interpretation of the four-error category.
* Record override reasons. An override does not recalculate the automated decision.
* Confirm every administered item has usable evidence and a reviewed outcome.
* Follow the study's export and storage instructions. Do not place recordings or
  identifiers in documentation, issue trackers, or public repositories.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 35 65

   * - Event
     - Researcher action
   * - Child speaks early or revises an answer
     - Keep recording; preserve the speech and review the final response.
   * - M does not audibly speak
     - Do not mark delivery as completed. Document the failure and follow the condition's recovery procedure.
   * - Microphone or browser stops
     - Preserve the available recording, document the gap, and use the approved interruption procedure. Do not describe a recovered file as uninterrupted capture.
   * - Local-storage warning
     - Keep the tab open and follow recovery instructions; the current tab may hold audio that was not saved locally.
   * - Analysis fails
     - Retain the same full recording and retry analysis after addressing the service problem.
   * - Archive upload fails
     - Retain the session, retry, and use approved audio/log exports as needed. Do not treat browser storage as a completed upload.
