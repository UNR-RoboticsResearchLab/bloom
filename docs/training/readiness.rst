5. Readiness review
===================

Use this page for self-study and an observed trainer review. The suggested
completion standard is demonstration of the critical workflow and correction
of all missed questions. The study lead sets the actual clearance criteria;
the RSR manual does not prescribe this training assessment or certify a trainee.

Self-check
----------

1. The child speaks before the prompt. Should recording begin only when the
   researcher opens the response window?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. Continuous recording must already be running before instructions and prompts. Response windows select material for analysis; they do not control capture.</p></details>

2. M's request says it was queued. May the researcher immediately mark the
   prompt as finished?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. Listen for actual playback and its audible end. A queue acknowledgement is not playback completion.</p></details>

3. The child requests a second replay of a scored sentence after its one
   allowed repetition was already used. Bloom permits another click. What now?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>The software does not enforce the manual's limit. Do not provide another repetition under the manual procedure. Follow a different rule only if the study has explicitly approved and documented that modification.</p></details>

4. A correct initial answer becomes an incorrect final answer. Which is scored?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>The child's final answer. Do not select the earlier correct response or the highest-scoring attempt.</p></details>

5. The child still gives no response after the prescribed memory prompt. Is this
   the same as having no recording of an item?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. A genuine no response after the prompt is recorded in the manual's zero-point category. An unadministered or inaudibly recorded item is missing evidence and must be documented separately.</p></details>

6. ASR counts a stuttering-related repetition and an appropriate contraction as
   errors. Must its item score be accepted?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. Review the recording using manual p. 4, which accepts appropriate contractions and does not count dysfluency-related repetitions. Apply a justified score override if needed, retaining the automatic result.</p></details>

7. The first several responses are incorrect. Can remaining items be skipped
   because the child has already performed poorly?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. The manual calls for administering all 16 items regardless of response accuracy. Other reasons for stopping follow the study's procedures and produce a documented incomplete session.</p></details>

8. The reviewed total changes after an override. Has the displayed automated
   screening decision necessarily changed too?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. Bloom preserves the automatic decision. Follow the approved review and reporting procedure rather than presenting it as recalculated from the reviewed total.</p></details>

9. The age form accepts a child aged 9 years 8 months. Does that establish a
   matching conversion table in the supplied manual?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. The supplied tables stop at 9 years 5 months. Form acceptance is not proof of eligibility or normative coverage.</p></details>

10. Audio is saved in this browser after the session. Is the server archive
    therefore complete?

.. raw:: html

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary><p>No. Select Save archive to Bloom, confirm the archive-saved event, and check retrieval. Browser recovery storage and the server archive are different.</p></details>

Observed demonstration
----------------------

The trainer records whether the researcher can:

* Locate the current condition instructions and identify any approved manual adaptations.
* Sign in with the assigned researcher account and choose the correct participant and condition.
* Start capture before the introduction, practice, and item prompts.
* Confirm the child display connection and audible delivery in the assigned mode.
* Mark prompt/response boundaries while keeping audio capture continuous.
* Enforce the manual's repeat allowance and document why a repetition occurred.
* Retain early speech and use the final response, including self-corrections.
* Administer every item or document a justified incomplete session.
* Handle no response, interruptions, and missing audio as distinct events.
* Apply the manual's scoring exceptions and explain the four-error wording issue.
* Correct boundaries before analysis and retain the basis for score overrides.
* Save and retrieve the archive without exposing participant data in training materials.
* Use the study's stopping, support, and incident-reporting procedures.

Trainer record
--------------

Keep the completed record in the study's designated training-record location,
not in this public documentation site. A suggested record is::

   Researcher:
   Trainer:
   Date:
   Manual/version used:
   Study protocol/version used:
   Conditions rehearsed:
   Rehearsal session IDs:
   Knowledge-check items requiring review:
   Observed steps requiring further practice:
   Outcome: ready for specified conditions / further training required
   Conditions or limitations on clearance:
   Researcher acknowledgement:
   Trainer sign-off:

Recheck readiness when the condition procedure, scoring interpretation, recording
setup, or software workflow changes materially.
