3. Review, score, and save
==========================

Review before automatic analysis
--------------------------------

After the last response and approved closing, select **Finish recording**.
Listen to the recording and check completeness. Confirm which items were actually
administered and which response is final for each item.

The full recording includes early speech, prompts, intervening speech, and breaks.
Automatic scoring uses marked response windows, not the entire interaction as a
single response. Before analysis, use **Correct response window** when a marked
boundary needs adjustment. Enter start and end in seconds from recording start
and supply a reason. Excluding an attempt also requires a reason.

Window corrections and exclusions are locked after automatic analysis succeeds.
Check them first. Never exclude an attempt merely because it lowers the score.

Score with the manual
---------------------

Manual p. 3 describes recording omissions, repetitions, additions, transpositions,
and substitutions. Manual p. 4 explains which differences count as scoring errors.
These are related activities: marking a repeated word does not mean counting a
stuttering-related repetition as an error.

Apply these manual rules to the child's final answer:

* Count a changed, added, substituted, or omitted word as one error.
* Count a transposition that changes sentence meaning as two errors; count a
  transposition that does not change meaning as one error.
* Accept an appropriate contraction or its expanded form without an error.
* Accept regional or cultural dialect forms when they belong to the child's
  language system; do not automatically penalize differences from mainstream
  American English.
* Do not count repetitions associated with dysfluency or stuttering as errors.

.. list-table:: Item points from manual p. 4
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 55 45

   * - Response
     - Points
   * - No scoring errors
     - 2
   * - One to three scoring errors
     - 1
   * - Four or more scoring errors, following the table's explanatory text
     - 0

.. important:: Four-error wording in the source

   The manual labels its zero-point column ``> 4 Errors`` but describes that
   category as **four or more** errors. The training examples follow the
   explanatory text and flag this inconsistency explicitly. The study lead should
   record the interpretation used in :doc:`protocol`; do not silently interpret
   four errors as an unscored gap or award one point.

The maximum raw total is 32 points across 16 scored items. Distinguish a genuine
no response after the manual's prompt from an unadministered item or a recording
failure. Missing evidence is not evidence of a zero-point response.

Do not delegate the final decision to ASR
-----------------------------------------

Select **Analyze response windows** only after checking the marked windows.
Transcription can mishear a word, include a prompt, combine self-corrections, or
penalize acceptable language differences. Compare the displayed transcript and
score with the original audio and the manual.

When an item needs a different score:

1. Select its item number.
2. Enter **Reason for override or exclusion** with the relevant evidence and rule.
3. Select **Reviewed score**, then **Apply score override**.
4. Check that the reviewed score and reason are correct.

Bloom retains the automatic score and transcript. It has no transcript-editing
control: when the displayed transcript does not represent the final answer,
describe the discrepancy and the reviewed final response in the session notes
or override reason. A score override does not recalculate the automated
screening decision. Do not report that decision as if it had incorporated the
reviewed total.

Age and interpretation
----------------------

Manual pp. 9-13 provide age-specific conversion tables. The last supplied band
is **9 years 0 months through 9 years 5 months**. Bloom's current entry form
accepts 60-119 months, extending beyond those supplied tables. Do not extrapolate
norms to 9 years 6-11 months, infer eligibility from the form's acceptance, or
choose a conversion from a different band. Confirm the study's applicable ages,
cutoff, and reporting procedure with the lead.

The manual's norming sample and accuracy information are described on pp. 5-8.
This training does not establish the validity of altered delivery conditions or
turn a screening output into a diagnosis.

Save a complete record
----------------------

1. Document interruptions, repeats, nonresponses, incomplete items, and review decisions.
2. Select **Save archive to Bloom**. Wait for completion and check the timeline
   for **archive saved**. Local browser storage alone is not a server archive.
3. If the study's storage procedure calls for exports, use **Download full audio**
   and **Download session log**, then store them only in the designated location.
4. If further review changes the record, save the archive again to create a new
   revision. Earlier archive revisions are retained.
5. From setup, use **Load saved archives** to check that the saved session is
   retrievable. Archives are scoped to the researcher who saved them.
6. Sign out after saving and completing the study's handoff procedure.

An analysis or archive failure does not justify making a new recording over the
old one. Keep the original session available, retry the failed operation, and
export the audio and log if instructed by the study's recovery procedure.
