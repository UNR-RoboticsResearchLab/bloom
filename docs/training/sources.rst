Training sources and scope
==========================

Source manual
-------------

Sean Redmond, *Redmond Sentence Recall*. The user-supplied copy is identified by
``R012011_RSRManual_20210310.pdf``. The original remains in the local RSR skills
folder and is not distributed through GitHub Pages. Filename dates identify the
supplied copy; they do not establish that it is the latest available edition.

The trainer and researcher should have access to their authorized source copy.
This training paraphrases the relevant procedures and uses the manual's brief
no-response prompt where exact wording matters.

.. list-table:: Where to verify a rule
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Printed page
     - Content used in this training
   * - 2
     - Task, original materials, practice, repeats, no-response prompt, all-item administration, and the final-answer rule.
   * - 3
     - Recording response differences with editing symbols.
   * - 4
     - Error counting, transpositions, contractions, dialect, dysfluency-related repetitions, item points, and raw total.
   * - 5-8
     - Norming-sample context, reliability, age scores, and accuracy information; not evidence validating Bloom's modified delivery conditions.
   * - 9-13
     - Age-specific standard-score and percentile conversion tables, ending at 9 years 5 months.

The manual has a wording inconsistency on p. 4: its zero-point column heading
says ``> 4 Errors`` while the explanatory text says four or more. This training
identifies both and uses the explanatory text in its exercises, pending the
study lead's documented interpretation.

Software source
---------------

Button names, recording behavior, response-window selection, researcher access,
and archive behavior were checked against Bloom's ``rsr-study-dashboard``
implementation. See :doc:`the administration reference </docs/rsr-study>`.
These describe current application behavior, not additional rules from the RSR
manual. If the software changes, review the corresponding training steps.

Study-specific source still needed
----------------------------------

The supplied manual does not define this study's three conditions or establish
approval for replacing its original auditory setup with examiner, browser, or M
administration. The study lead supplies those instructions and completes
:doc:`protocol`. Rehearsal exercises, knowledge questions, and the suggested
training schedule are training aids created for Bloom, not author-provided RSR
certification requirements.
